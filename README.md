A fun mini-game where you play as a cube armed with purple bananas! Your goal is to defeat the evil spikes and survive as long as you can.  This game was originally created based on the ideas of a Grade 1 child, a friend and I turned their ideas into a playable game with the help of our cs teacher Ms. Courtney Edwards and her tutorial.
This was originally ran with Thonny, I am not sure if it works with anything else, it probably should though. 
There is also a reference tracker for all my references.
I also have a video of the walkthrough of how to play this game in this link: https://youtu.be/4pfcdFjXKJk.
